1. About Tailor Master & Core Purpose

Tailor Master (Package Name: com.himanshupatel.tailorpro) is a professional tailoring business management application built for custom tailoring shops, garment designers, boutique owners, master cutters, and stitchers.

The application serves as a dedicated digital workspace to record customer garment fittings and measurements, issue tailoring orders, allocate tasks to specialized workers, monitor order fulfillment stages in real-time, manage material inventory, and calculate payments and due balances.

We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and privacy of all data recorded by tailoring shop owners and their customers. This document details precisely what information is collected, why it is needed, how it is secured, and how you can manage or delete your data at any time.

Section 2

2. Detailed App Workflows: What the App Does

To provide full transparency for users and Google Play Reviewers, here is the complete operational lifecycle of how Tailor Master operates:

1 User Registration & Shop Setup

Tailors, shop owners, and garment workers register an account to access the shop's management portal.

  • Phone Number: Acts as the primary account identifier for login and account security.
  • Full Name & Business Name: Identifies the tailoring shop and displays on invoices and worker assignments.
  • Shop Address: Printed on customer receipts and order invoices.
  • Password: Hashed and salted using industry-standard bcrypt encryption before storage.
  • Role Assignment: Users register as either Admin (Shop Owner) or User (Tailor / Worker).

2 Administrator Verification & Approval Policy

To ensure data protection and prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing confidential customer measurements or shop sales data:

  • Newly registered user accounts default to a Pending Approval state.
  • The Shop Administrator inspects the pending account in the in-app Admin Panel.
  • Only after explicit Admin verification and approval can the user view shop orders, customer profiles, or worker tasks.
  • Admins can also assign subscription validity or deactivate worker access if personnel leaves the shop.

3 Secure Role-Based Login & Access

Approved personnel access the system with their Phone Number and Password:

  • Shop Owners / Admins: Full access to financial metrics, worker approvals, inventory, pricing, and all customer orders.
  • Tailors & Workers: Scoped access limited only to orders and measurement sheets assigned to them for cutting, stitching, or finishing.

4 Customer Measurement Management

The app enables shopkeepers to build a permanent fitting record for repeat customers:

  • Customer Profile: Full name, contact phone number, address, and gender.
  • Custom Garment Measurements: Precise body measurements for shirts, suits, trousers, kurtas, jackets, and blouses (e.g., chest, waist, hips, shoulder, sleeve length, collar, neck, inseam, cuff size).
  • Fitting History: Updating and revisiting previous fitting measurements for future tailoring orders without remeasuring.

5 Order Creation Flow ("Make Order")

When a client places an order, the user records complete order specifications:

  • Garment & Quantity: Selected item types (Shirt, Suit, Pant, Kurta, Safari, etc.) and quantities.
  • Design & Customization: Collar styles, cuff designs, pocket placement, pleats, and button styles.
  • Fabric & Design Photos: Uploading or capturing cloth pattern photos or customer reference sample pictures using the device camera or gallery.
  • Stitching Notes: Written or audio instructions detailing special customer tailoring preferences.
  • Billing & Advance Deposit: Subtotal, discounts, advance deposit amount paid, and calculated pending balance due.
  • Delivery Scheduling: Promised trial and delivery dates.

6 Worker Task Assignment & Progress Tracking ("Track Work")

Ensures systematic fulfillment of orders and team accountability:

Pending ➔ Cutting ➔ Stitching ➔ Finishing ➔ Quality Check ➔ Ready for Delivery ➔ Delivered
  • Job Splitting: The shop owner assigns individual items or skills (e.g., Cutting, Stitching) to specific workers.
  • Real-Time Updates: Workers update status as they complete their assigned phase of the garment.
  • Delivery Readiness: Notifications and alerts alert shop staff when an order is ready for customer pickup.

7 Material Inventory & Stock Control

The app tracks raw materials required for tailoring:

  • Fabric rolls (meters), buttons, threads, zippers, lining, canvas, and accessories.
  • Supplier information and restock quantity alerts.

8 Invoicing, Due Payments & Receipts

Transparent accounting for both tailor and customer:

  • Invoice & Receipt Generation: Automatically formats order bills with shop branding, customer details, measurements, item list, and balance due.
  • Document Sharing: Generates printable PDF receipts that can be shared via WhatsApp, SMS, or printer.
  • Due Balance Follow-up: Filter orders with unpaid balances to streamline receivables collection upon garment pickup.

Section 5

5. Android Device Permissions & Justifications

The app requests the following runtime permissions strictly to support user-initiated actions:

android.permission.CAMERA

Reason: Used when the user chooses to take a live photo of a client's cloth fabric swatch, embroidery sample, or custom garment styling directly inside the Order Creation screen.

android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Reason: Used when the user selects an existing photo of a garment design or fabric sample from the device photo gallery to attach to an order.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Reason: Used to display local and push notifications alerting the tailor or worker about upcoming order delivery dates, pending approvals, or assignment changes.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Reason: Used to transmit encrypted data between the mobile app and the secure MongoDB backend API.

Section 6

6. Data Protection, Storage & Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security practices to safeguard all business and customer information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the mobile client and backend servers is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) over HTTPS.
  • Password Protection: Passwords are protected using one-way bcrypt cryptographic hashing with random salt rounds. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): JSON Web Tokens (JWT) authenticate every API request. Tailors and workers cannot access admin financial reports or unauthorized shop settings.
  • Cloud Database Security: Backend databases are hosted on enterprise-grade MongoDB Atlas with network firewalls and restricted IP access.
  • Local Storage: Authentication tokens and shop session states stored locally on the device use isolated application storage (AsyncStorage) inaccessible to other apps.

7. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliant)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies regarding account and data deletion, users have the permanent right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal and business data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated records through either of the following methods:

  1. Direct In-App Deletion:
    Open the Tailor Master app → Navigate to Settings → Scroll to Actions → Tap Clear All Data or Delete Account. Confirm the prompt to immediately remove your session and clear local records.
  2. Direct Email Deletion Request:
    Send an email from or referencing your registered phone number to:
    cloudiefyy@gmail.com
    Include the following details:
    • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Tailor Master
    • Your Registered Phone Number
    • Your Registered Shop / Business Name

Data Deletion Timeline & Scope

Upon receiving your confirmed deletion request:

  • Your user profile, login credentials, customer measurement sheets, order archives, and attached photos will be permanently deleted from our live databases within 7 business days.
  • Any database backup snapshots containing your data are automatically overwritten within 30 days.
  • This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your fitting histories and order records cannot be recovered.
